# Sovereign Partner Operating Infrastructure (SYS-MOD-PARTNER-001)
**Penn Enterprises LLC Operational Standard**

## Executive Purpose

This framework governs how Keymon Penn (Owner & CEO) onboards, equips, supervises, and executes business with a close operating partner (such as Derrick McCullough).

In high growth enterprises, bringing in a close partner is the highest leverage move a founder can make. However, vague delegation and undefined boundaries inevitably lead to missed deadlines, confusion, and strained relationships. This infrastructure establishes clear boundaries, sandboxed tooling, a 4 tier authority ladder, and shared commercial incentives so both partners move with extreme speed, high trust, and shared revenue.

## 1. The 4 Tier Partner Authority Ladder

Authority is earned through verified outputs, never assumed:

• **Tier 1 (A0: Shadow & Sandbox):**
Partner builds and executes inside local sandboxes or staging environments. Keymon reviews outputs before any client or external exposure.

• **Tier 2 (A1: Supervised Live Execution):**
Partner handles real client workflows or production tasks, but Keymon provides final 1 tap approval before public transmission or live deployment.

• **Tier 3 (A2: Bounded Domain Ownership):**
Partner completely owns a specific operational domain (e.g., Video & Media Production, Client Intake, or Lead Curation) within pre-agreed weekly volume and quality standards.

• **Tier 4 (A3: Commercial Co-Pilot):**
Partner co-pitches, closes, and delivers full client retainers with agreed revenue splits.

## 2. The Partner Workstation & Tooling Architecture

To execute without dependency bottlenecks, the partner must be equipped with dedicated tools:

• **Dedicated Git Repositories:** Cloned templates under their own GitHub account, maintaining clean version control.
• **Sandboxed Sub-Accounts:** Sub-accounts on GoHighLevel, Twilio, or Vapi rather than sharing master root credentials.
• **Agentic IDE Configuration:** Cursor, Antigravity, or VS Code loaded with Penn Enterprises verified skills.
• **Shared Cloud Ingestion Bin:** A dedicated Google Drive, Dropbox, or iCloud folder for dropping raw assets and receiving final exports.

## 3. The Task Handshake Protocol (Zero Vague Work)

Every delegated task between partners must satisfy the 4 Point Handshake:

1. Objective: What does done look like in 1 clear sentence?
2. Assets: Exactly where do the raw files live?
3. Delivery Target: Exact folder or URL destination.
4. Stop Condition: Deadline timestamp and verification check.

## 4. Division of Powers & Commercial Revenue Model

To avoid operational collisions, responsibilities are clearly divided:

• **Keymon Penn (Lead Systems Architect & CEO):**
Owns core systems architecture, high ticket client acquisition, contract negotiation, and master control plane governance.

• **Operating Partner (Operations & Fulfillment Lead):**
Owns media production, client onboarding fulfillment, automation testing, and operational execution.

• **Commercial Economics:**
Clear revenue splits on joint client retainers (e.g., 50/50 on delivery retainers, or 70/30 based on origination versus fulfillment) tracked transparently in a shared ledger.
