# Video Replication & Hook Remaster Skill

## Executive Summary
This skill empowers Derrick's AI agent to analyze high performing reference videos, deconstruct their hook mechanics and pacing cadence, and generate matching production storyboards for short form content.

## The 4 Gear Content Flywheel
1. Hook Deconstruction: Break down the first 3 seconds into visual hook, verbal hook, and on screen text banner.
2. Pacing Grid Analysis: Map visual cuts and camera punch ins every 2.5 to 3.5 seconds.
3. Retention Anchor Points: Identify where visual artifacts (documents, stats, screenshots) appear to maintain engagement.
4. Call to Action Blueprint: Lock in the final 5 second closing command with clear action steps.

## Execution Runbook
• Step 1: Ingest reference video URL or local MP4 take.
• Step 2: Extract spoken transcript and timestamped audio cues.
• Step 3: Map visual change intervals (average cut length between 2.2s and 3.5s).
• Step 4: Output structured script beats with recommended A-Roll cuts and B-Roll overlays.
